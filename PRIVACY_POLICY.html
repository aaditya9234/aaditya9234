Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the GPS Map Camera mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application", "App", or "Service") created by the developer ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information our Application accesses, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

Key Summary: GPS Map Camera operates primarily offline. All photos, geolocation coordinates, and field project notes are processed locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers or sold to third parties.

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

A. Location Data (Precise and Approximate)

The Application requests access to your device's ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION permissions.

  • Purpose: To acquire your real-time GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy) and address to imprint visual geolocation stamps and EXIF metadata directly onto the captured photographs.
  • Storage & Transfer: Location data is used strictly in real-time during photo capture and is embedded locally onto the generated photo file on your device. We do not track your location in the background when the app is closed, and we do not transmit or store your location data on any external servers.

B. Camera and Audio

The Application requests CAMERA and RECORD_AUDIO permissions.

  • Purpose: To display the live camera viewfinder, adjust focus, zoom, and capture photos with embedded GPS data overlays. Audio permission is optional for video capture features.
  • Storage & Transfer: All image captures are processed on-device and saved directly to your local media storage.

C. Storage and Media Access

The Application requests READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permissions.

  • Purpose: To save captured photos to your device gallery, display thumbnail previews in the app's Field Gallery, export PDF reports, and enable user-directed sharing.
  • Storage & Transfer: The app only accesses files created by the application or selected directly by you for viewing or exporting.

D. Biometrics / Fingerprint Sensor (Optional)

The Application includes an optional hardware shutter feature utilizing USE_BIOMETRIC or USE_FINGERPRINT.

  • Purpose: To allow you to press the physical fingerprint scanner as an alternative camera shutter button.
  • Privacy Notice: The Application never collects, views, or stores your fingerprint or biometric data. It relies solely on Android's standard key event signals.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development and offer core features for free, the Application integrates Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable device data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • IP address and approximate network-level location
  • Ad interaction events (views, impressions, and clicks)

For more information on how Google collects and uses your data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads.

3. Data Retention and Security

Because all photos, project profiles, and GPS stamps remain on your device, you maintain full control over your data. Deleting photos from the Field Gallery or clearing app storage permanently deletes the respective records from your device.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The Application is not intended for or directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) / GDPR (EU Users)

Under CCPA and GDPR, users have the right to request information about data collection, access, deletion, and opt-out of personal data sales. Because we do not store your personal information on our servers or sell your data, your rights are fully respected by default.
